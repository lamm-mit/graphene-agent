The explored design space (Stages 1-2): baselines (pristine, vacancies, cracks, hole, reference mesh), the hierarchy ladder (single-scale meshes with three periods, coarse level alone, two-level meshes with three scale ratios and three vein widths, three-level mesh) at matched porosity 0.20, disorder (jitter, size dispersion, Voronoi networks of different regularity and cell count), anisotropy and load-path organisation (slits at 0/45/90 degrees, elliptical pores, strut lattices with and without a strut family along the load), porosity sweep, gradients, and flaw halos. N = atoms, phi = porosity, H = designed hierarchy depth. Loading is along x (horizontal).
