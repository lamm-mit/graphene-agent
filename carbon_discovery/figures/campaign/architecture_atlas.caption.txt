Atlas of all 96 simulated architectures of the discovery campaign: relaxed structure of one periodic cell (atoms as black dots), grouped by generator family (frame colour, legend at the bottom) and ordered by decreasing 2D strength within each family. Below each structure: 2D strength sigma (N/m), work to failure W (J/m$^2$, proxy), failure strain, porosity phi, number of atoms N and fracture-mode class (abrupt = single avalanche, stepwise = few events, progressive = multiple events with load retained). Model results (screened REBO2, athermal quasi-static tension).
