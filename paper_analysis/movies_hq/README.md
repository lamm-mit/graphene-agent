# Fracture movies (supplementary material)

MP4 (H.264, 2560x1440 masters at 30 fps; `*_1080p.mp4` are compact 1920x1080 versions for supplementary information). The movies contain no text other than the axis labels of the chart and the numbers on the colour bar; this file and `captions.json` carry the captions. Layout: the structures (left to right in the order of the table below; the frame colour of each structure is the colour of its curve) above a chart of 2D stress against engineering strain with a moving marker per structure and a dashed line at the current strain; the vertical bar at the right is the colour scale (an arrow means that values above the range saturate). All panels advance at the same engineering strain (uniform rate, slowed 3× around first damage, peak load and major propagation); a panel whose simulation has ended holds its final state. Frames between the stored quasi-static states are interpolated (positions, cell and per-atom energies; bonds recomputed with the 2.0 Å criterion), so intermediate configurations are interpolations, not simulated states. Colour: energy change per atom relative to the relaxed state (inferno colour map) on a scale shared by the panels of a movie and set by their pre-peak strain energy; red rings: atoms that have lost a bond; cells smaller than 60 Å are shown as 2×2 periodic images. Model results (screened REBO2, athermal quasi-static tension). Generated by `paper_analysis/make_movies.py` from the experiment database.

## M01: Baselines: intrinsic strength and a single flaw

`M01_baselines_pristine_vs_precrack.mp4` (38.5 s). The pristine zigzag sheet reaches 38.8 N/m and fails in one avalanche; a 20 Å crack halves the strength (18.5 N/m) and fails by crack propagation at 9% strain.

Colour scale -0.01 to 0.48 eV per atom.

## M02: Tilting a load-aligned slit array: three mechanisms, one minimum

## M04: Hierarchy is not a free lunch: alignment, level by level

`M04_hierarchy_vs_alignment.mp4` (48.0 s). All at porosity 0.20. The single-level square mesh (17.0 N/m) beats the nested mesh with veins both ways and round pores (12.8). Aligning the veins with the load (16.0) or elongating the fine pores along it (17.5) recovers the loss; doing both gives the strongest nested design (19.4), because each level then adds straight load paths.

Colour scale -1.22 to 0.88 eV per atom.

| veins along load | `S4_H2_D40_W8_veins_x` | `run_20260905_172505_71282e` | 16.0 | 0.189 | progressive (multiple events, load retained) |

## M06: Disorder and gradients are costs

`M06_disorder_and_gradients_are_costs.mp4` (44.2 s). Same porosity as the ordered fine mesh (11.7 N/m for this lattice registry, 11.7 to 13.3 across registries): positional jitter of 2 Å (11.8 for this seed, 10.4 to 11.8 across seeds), pore-size dispersion (11.3), a Voronoi network (14.4 for this seed, 9.2 to 14.4 across seeds) and a pore-size gradient along the load (10.1, set by its weakest band). None is a toughening mechanism in this model.

Colour scale -0.64 to 0.52 eV per atom.

## M08: Fracture mode is a load-transfer property

`M08_fracture_modes_progressive_vs_avalanche.mp4` (37.4 s). Eight parallel strips (armchair slit array) fail row by row and keep carrying 8 to 10 N/m to 25% strain; three wide rows with 15% width dispersion cascade in one avalanche at 14% strain. Many parallel paths of moderate dispersion share the released load; few wide paths cannot.

## M09: Close-up: en-echelon linking of overlapping slit tips (20°)

`M09_closeup_en_echelon_linking.mp4` (29.2 s). The short inclined ligaments between the tips of slits in adjacent rows carry the load in combined tension and shear and fail first; each failed ligament hands its load to the next one along the same diagonal, so the cracks link across the sheet in a staircase while the remaining strips keep carrying about 8 N/m.

## M10: Close-up: the strongest nested design (veins and pores along the load)

`M10_closeup_aligned_hierarchy.mp4` (32.2 s). Fine pores elongated along the load inside veins that run along the load: 19.4 N/m at porosity 0.21. First damage appears at the bridges between the fine pores, the peak follows, and the fine-pore rows fail in a few steps while the veins carry the remaining load. A holdout that the data-driven predictor underestimated by a third.

Colour scale -1.22 to 0.83 eV per atom.
